# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (responsive: desktop and phone carry equal weight)

## Users
- **Sócias (admin):** Iata de Almeida and Larissa de Almeida, founders of LI Projetos. See everything, manage users and the price table, watch performance.
- **Equipe:** team members who answer leads and send proposals; they see only clients assigned to them.
- Usage is split evenly between the office computer and the phone (between meetings, answering a lead quickly).

## Product Purpose
Internal CRM for LI Projetos, a 100% online architecture and interior design studio serving the Distrito Federal and Goiânia (no construction work). It catches every lead from liprojetos.com.br (forms, meeting requests, WhatsApp and "Agendar" clicks, with origin and Google Ads gclid), runs the funnel Novo → Contato → Reunião → Proposta → Fechado/Perdido, builds proposals from the official price table (PDF, e-mail, WhatsApp, public accept link) and shows sales performance. Success: no lead is lost, first contact is fast, and the partners can see which channel brings contracts.

## Constraints
- PHP 8.3, no framework, Bootstrap 5.3 served locally; strict CSP (no inline scripts or style attributes, no CDNs).
- Hosted on Hostinger shared hosting (crm.liprojetos.com.br); local development in Docker.
- Language: pt-BR throughout.
- Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, 44px touch targets.

## Brand Commitments
- Name always spelled "LI Projetos".
- Use the studio's own logo (logo-li-projetos.svg / logo-li-projetos-light.svg from the website).
- Three user-selectable themes: light, dark, and LI Projetos brand (website colours: cream #FAF8F5, ink #1A1918, gold #C5A059 / gold-dark #866628).
